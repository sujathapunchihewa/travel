About Lanka Travellers

Passionate locals dedicated to sharing the true spirit of Sri Lanka with the world.

Who We Are

Born from a Love for Sri Lanka

Lanka Travellers was founded by a group of travel enthusiasts and local guides who wanted to offer something different — genuine experiences that go beyond the typical tourist trail.

We believe travel should be meaningful. That’s why we carefully design every itinerary to connect visitors with Sri Lanka’s rich culture, warm people, stunning landscapes and living heritage, while supporting local communities and protecting the environment.

From the moment you enquire to the day you leave, our team is with you — ensuring smooth logistics, insider knowledge, and the kind of hospitality that makes Sri Lanka unforgettable.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

🔭 Our Vision

To be Sri Lanka’s most trusted and inspiring travel partner — recognised for authentic experiences, responsible practices, and creating lifelong memories for every traveller who chooses us.

🎯 Our Mission

To craft personalised, high-quality journeys that showcase the real Sri Lanka, empower local communities, respect nature, and deliver exceptional service with heart and professionalism.
